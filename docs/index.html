Where to use it

  • Web app, on a computer or a phone: cloud.pallino.me. To install it, use the install icon in the address bar (Chrome, Edge), or “Add to Home screen” in the menu of your phone’s browser.
  • Android app: coming soon on Google Play.

No account is needed: what you write stays on the device, and works offline.

Write a task

Write it as you would say it, in English or in Italian. For example:

Send the draft tomorrow at 10:00 !! #thesis &Thesis

You writePallino reads
tomorrow Friday at 6 pmThe day and the time
by FridayA deadline
every MondayA task that repeats
!!! !! !Urgent, high or medium priority
#thesisA tag
&ThesisA project
@LucaThe person it is for
~1h30How long it takes
;tomorrowA reminder

Notes

Notes are Markdown: headings, lists, - [ ] to-dos, **bold**, ==highlights==, tables, code, $formulas$ and > [!TIP] callouts. Lists and bookmarks are in Notes too.

Spaces

A space keeps together the tasks, notes and projects of one part of your life, in its own colour.

Pallino Cloud

An optional account that keeps tasks, notes, projects and spaces in step across your devices. With it you can share a space by inviting people with a link, or with a QR code from Android.

Shortcuts

In the web app on a computer, press ? to see them all. N adds, S chooses the space, Ctrl K searches.
