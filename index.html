JUST A FEW QUESTIONS.

Before you start.

Do I need an account to use Pallino?+

You can start in offline mode with a local profile. Pallino Cloud is optional: it syncs your profile, spaces and projects across devices, while tasks and notes stay on your phone.

Can I write tasks in English or Italian?+

Yes, both. Pallino recognises expressions like “tomorrow”, “Friday at 6 pm”, “by Friday”, “every Monday” and “~1h30”, in English and in Italian. The app interface is in English.

Is AI required?+

No. You can manage tasks, notes and lists and use rule-based extraction without any AI. For AI extraction you can download a local model to your phone (LiteRT-LM) or use Google AI Studio with your own key.

Can I take my notes with me?+

Of course. You can export all notes as Markdown (`.md`) or in the `.pallino` format, and export or re-import a full `.json` backup from the Profile settings.
