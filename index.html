JUST A FEW QUESTIONS.

Before you start.

Do I need an account to use Pallino?+

No. You can start with a local profile, on Android and on the web, and everything stays on that device. Pallino Cloud is optional: with an account, your tasks, notes, projects and spaces stay in step across phone and computer, and you can share a space with other people.

Where can I use Pallino?+

In any modern browser at cloud.pallino.me, on a computer or on a phone, and in the Android app, coming soon on Google Play.

How do I install the web app?+

Open cloud.pallino.me. In Chrome or Edge on a computer, use the install icon in the address bar. On Android, open the Chrome menu and choose “Add to Home screen”. On iPhone, tap Share in Safari and then “Add to Home Screen”.

Is the web app the same as the Android app?+

Tasks, notes, lists, spaces, search, templates and Pallino Cloud are in both. Focus and its daily review, dictation, AI, adding pictures and reminders with the app closed are on Android.

Can I write tasks in English or Italian?+

Yes, both. Pallino recognises expressions like “tomorrow”, “Friday at 6 pm”, “by Friday”, “every Monday” and “~1h30”, in English and in Italian. The interface is in English.

Is AI required?+

No. You can manage tasks, notes and lists and extract tasks by rules (on Android) without any AI. For AI extraction, on Android, you can download a local model to your phone (LiteRT-LM) or use Google AI Studio with your own key.

Can I take my notes with me?+

Of course. You can export all notes as Markdown (`.md`) or in the `.pallino` format, and export or re-import a full `.json` backup from the Profile settings. Any note can also be printed or saved as PDF.
